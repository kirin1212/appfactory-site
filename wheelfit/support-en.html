Questions

Does a check mean the car passes inspection?
No. The checks are estimates from the numbers you enter. Always check on the car before you buy or fit. The rules used and their sources are listed in the app under "Calculations and Standards".

Where do I measure?
See "How to Measure" in the app. Measure with the current wheels, the car empty and the wheels straight.

The tire width differs from the real tire.
Without a total width, the app estimates it from the size and rim width. Enter the catalog total width for a more exact result.

Free and full version?
Free: one car, two plans, the changes, the drawing, the speedometer and fitment checks. The full version (one-time in-app purchase) adds unlimited cars and plans, room left from your measurements, front and rear sizes, spacers, camber and lowering.

New iPhone?
Tap "Restore Purchase" with the same Apple Account. Move your cars with Settings › Export Cars.
