Privacy Policy

Wheel Fitment: Offset & Poke (WheelFit) · Last updated 23 October 2026

Information collected

This app does not collect any personal information or data. There is no account or sign-in.

Stored on your device

The cars, wheel and tire sizes, measurements and plans you enter are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere. Backup files and share images are created only when you choose where to save or send them.

Permissions

The app does not use the camera, photos, location or notifications.

In-app purchase

Purchases and restores go through Apple's App Store; payment details never reach the developer. See also Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ads, analytics and third parties

No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools are used. No information is shared with third parties.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Open a "New issue" on the GitHub Issues page (a free GitHub account is needed). Please start the title with "WheelFit".
