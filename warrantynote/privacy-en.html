Privacy Policy

Warranty Note / Last updated: October 16, 2026

Information we collect

This app does not collect any personal information or data. There is no account or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

The items you register (names, purchase dates, warranty terms, notes and so on), photos and manual PDFs are stored only on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Backup and CSV files are created only when you choose where to export them. You are responsible for where you keep those files.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you scan a warranty card or receipt. Photos are read only when you pick them in the system photo picker; the app never accesses your whole photo library.

Notifications

Warranty reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No server is used.

In-app purchase

Purchases and restores are handled by Apple's App Store. Your payment details are never shared with the developer. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles this information.

Ads, analytics and third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no information is shared with third parties.

Children

Anyone can use this app. Because it collects no information, it collects no information from children.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with the date of the update.

Contact

Please open a "New issue" on our GitHub support page (Issues). A free GitHub account is required.
