FAQ

What is the difference between the free and full versions?
The free version keeps up to 3 items. The full version (one-time in-app purchase) adds unlimited items, reminders before warranties end, manual PDFs and CSV export.

I got a new iPhone. What happens to my purchase?
Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap "Restore Purchase" in Settings or on the purchase screen.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?
Use "Export Backup" in Settings to save a file to the Files app (for example iCloud Drive), then choose "Import Backup" on the new iPhone. Photos and PDFs are included.

Reminders don't arrive.
Check that Reminders are on in the app's Settings, and that notifications are allowed for the app in iPhone Settings > Notifications.

Does the app need the internet?
Everything except buying and restoring the purchase works in Airplane Mode.
