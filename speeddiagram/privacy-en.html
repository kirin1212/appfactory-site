Privacy Policy

Speed Problems Visualized / Last updated: October 29, 2026

Information we collect

This app does not collect any personal information or data. There is no account or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

Your stars, the last problem you opened and the sound and vibration settings are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere. Backup files are created only when you choose where to save them.

Permissions

The app does not use the camera, photos, location or notifications.

In-app purchase

Purchases and restores are handled by Apple's App Store; payment details are never shared with the developer. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ads, analytics and third parties

No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools are used, and nothing is shared with third parties.

Children

Nothing is collected when children use the app either. Parents can manage in-app purchases with Apple's Ask to Buy.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with the date of the update.

Contact

Please open a "New issue" on our GitHub support page (Issues) and start the title with "Speed Problems". A free GitHub account is required.
