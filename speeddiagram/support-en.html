FAQ

What is the difference between the free and full versions?
Chapter 1 (20 problems) and the first five problems of chapter 2 are free. The full version (one-time purchase) adds the other 95 problems (meeting and catching up, ponds, trains, rivers and clocks) and Try It Yourself.

How do I type fractions and mixed numbers?
Tap the answer box to show the keys. Type a fraction as 60/11, and a mixed number as 5, the mixed number key, then 5/11. A decimal such as 2.5 is also right when it is the same value.

How do I read the diagram?
Time runs across and the place goes up. The slope of a line is the speed, and where two lines cross is where they meet. "How to Use" in the app explains more.

I am stuck.
Tap the light bulb (Hint) to see the solution one step at a time. Tap a step with ▶ to move the picture to that moment.

I got a new iPhone.
Tap "Restore Purchase" with the same Apple Account, and move your stars with "Save Stars to Files" and "Load Stars from Files" in Settings.
