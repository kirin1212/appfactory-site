FAQ

What is the difference between the free and full versions?
The free version plans one set with one item, with cut sizes, fabric length and the cutting layout. The full version (one-time purchase) adds unlimited sets and items, custom pieces, and PDF and image export.

How accurate is it?
Cut sizes are finished size plus seam allowances and hems. Pieces are laid out across the fabric width, and the length is rounded up to 10 cm with shrinkage and spare added (both adjustable in Settings). Pattern matching and fraying are not included, so treat it as a guide.

I got a new iPhone.
Tap "Restore Purchase" with the same Apple Account, and move your data with "Export Backup" in Settings.
