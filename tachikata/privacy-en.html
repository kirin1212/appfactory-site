Privacy Policy

Cut Planner / Last updated: October 17, 2026

Information we collect

This app does not collect any personal information or data. There is no account or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

Your sets, item sizes and fabric settings are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Backup files, PDFs and images are created only when you choose where to save them. You are responsible for where you keep them.

Permissions

The app does not use the camera, photos, location or notifications.

In-app purchase

Purchases and restores are handled by Apple's App Store; payment details are never shared with the developer. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ads, analytics and third parties

No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools are used, and nothing is shared with third parties.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with the date of the update.

Contact

Please open a "New issue" on our GitHub support page (Issues) and start the title with "Cut Planner". A free GitHub account is required.
