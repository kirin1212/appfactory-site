For bug reports and questions, please contact us below. Your iOS version, device and the puzzle number (for example 4-12) help a lot.

Please open a "New issue" on our GitHub support page (Issues) and start the title with "Block Views". A free GitHub account is required.

FAQ

What is the difference between the free and full versions?
Chapter 1 (20 puzzles) and the hints are free. The full version (one-time purchase) adds chapters 2 to 6 with 100 puzzles, including the most and fewest blocks puzzles.

Which way does the right view face?
It is seen from the right, so the near side of the board is on the left of the drawing and the far side on the right. The top view has the near side at the bottom, like the board.

The views match but the puzzle is not cleared.
Puzzles for the most or the fewest blocks clear when the views match and the count is the largest (or smallest) possible. If the game says more blocks fit or some can come off, add or remove blocks without changing the views.

I am stuck.
Tap the light bulb (Hint) to see one square of an answer at a time. Clear without a hint for three stars.

I got a new iPhone.
Tap "Restore Purchase" with the same Apple Account, and move your stars with "Save Progress to Files" and "Load Progress from Files" in Settings.
